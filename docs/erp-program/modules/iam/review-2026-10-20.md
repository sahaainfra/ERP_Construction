## Suspicions carried forward
- Time inputs (`now`, `at`, `asOf`) are accepted by the services. A route must not forward a time from the client. Recorded as a rule for the route layer.
- Segregation of duties covers only actions that modules record through `recordAction`.
- Shadow comparison results are supplied by the caller. Nothing calls it yet.

## Tests that now assert the right thing
The tests that passed for the wrong reason were corrected: lock and revocation messages, the no-record fallback, the review item count (which now includes tenant-wide grants), and the export test (which now uses real sessions). New tests cover elevation against a deny, revocation in the decision path, linked-account approval, approval without permission, malformed conditions, the MFA downgrade and wrong-code counting.
